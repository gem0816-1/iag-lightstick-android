## Artwork and publication changes

- The old eight Apple Music APK-derived vector files are excluded. Their
  resource names are retained for source compatibility, but their contents are
  new, simple geometric play/pause/skip/menu symbols.
- The four old artwork bitmaps are excluded. Original light-stick and launcher
  images and an HSV color wheel are generated by `tools/generate_public_assets.py`.
  These generated assets and the generator use GPL-3.0-only; their hashes are
  recorded in `docs/public-assets.json`. The generator reads no old image.
- The personal fixed BLE address and its address-specific ranking are removed;
  existing device-name/service matching and BLE frames are unchanged.
- The public variant's visible label is `IAG Light Stick`. The application ID,
  version, storage schema and signing configuration are unchanged.
- Unused historical glass sources, a concert-specific placeholder asset,
  local handoff/configuration, decompiled references, packet captures, old APKs,
  signing material and local build evidence are excluded from this repository.

This is a source-only publication, not an official hardware-vendor release.
Historical private-worktree APK hashes do not describe this variant's APK.
Device tests and Google Play approval have not been completed.
